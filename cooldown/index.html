Cooldown privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Cooldown stores everything you enter, including batch names, food names, start times, temperatures, readings and display preferences, only on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData. The developer does not operate servers for this app, does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking. The app does not require an account and makes no network requests. You can delete any batch or reading inside the app, and removing the app deletes all of its data from your device. If you have a question about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
