Pantry Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pantry does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The items you enter (names, quantities, categories, and expiry dates) are stored only on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Pantry does not create accounts, does not contact any server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing is sent off the device, there is no data for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer at the support address listed on the App Store page.
