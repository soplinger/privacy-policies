Tintwave Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Tintwave ("the app") is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad by Sean Oplinger. This policy explains what information the app uses and who it is shared with.

Information that stays on your device

Your scores, best chain, games played, the game in progress and your sound and haptics settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we do not collect this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner ad during play, short full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to revive a game or get new pieces). To serve ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and usage information, and approximate location. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads can be more relevant to you; if you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized by tracking. If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EU or UK), the app asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time from the settings menu under "Privacy & ad choices". You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your high score and biggest chain to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy. Signing in is optional, and the game works fully without it.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases and does not ask for payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
