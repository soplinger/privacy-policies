Nursling Privacy Policy

Nursling stores everything you enter (baby names, birth dates, feeds, diapers, notes and settings) only on your iPhone, using the device's local storage. Nursling does not create accounts, does not send data to any server or third party, does not use analytics or advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you turn on the feed reminder, Nursling asks iOS for permission to show notifications; those notifications are created and delivered on your device. Deleting the app deletes its data. You can delete any baby, feed or diaper record at any time from within the app. Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the App Store listing.
