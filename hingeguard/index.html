Hingeguard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Hingeguard ("the app") is a game for iPhone by Sean Oplinger. This policy explains what information the app uses and who it is shared with.

Information that stays on your device

Your scores, run history, settings, shards, upgrades and daily-challenge streak are stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we do not collect this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch and, in some versions, short full-screen ads between runs. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and usage information, and approximate location. If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EU or UK), the app asks for your choice first. You can change it any time in Settings, under Privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns which items you own so it can unlock them. The Remove Ads purchase turns off full-screen ads between runs.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your score to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
