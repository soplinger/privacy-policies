Beat Idol: Support

Need help with Beat Idol? Start here.

Common questions

The beat feels early or late. Open Settings in the game and move the beat timing offset. Bluetooth headphones usually need a higher value.

My purchase didn't show up. Open the Backstage Boutique, then Extras, and tap Restore Purchases.

Leaderboards and awards are greyed out. Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then reopen the game.

I want fewer flashes or less shaking. Turn on "Reduce flashing & screen shake" in Settings.

Ads. Rewarded ads are always optional. You can change tracking permissions in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Contact us

Open an issue at github.com/soplinger/privacy-policies/issues with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.
