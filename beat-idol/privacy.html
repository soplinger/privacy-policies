Beat Idol: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Beat Idol is a free-to-play rhythm survivor game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses and who handles it.

What stays on your device

Your progress (gold, unlocked stars and outfits, settings, best times and daily results) is saved locally on your device. We do not run our own servers, we have no user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads between runs using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and to personalize them if you allow it. This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EU, EEA or UK), the game shows Google's consent form before ads load.
  • On iOS, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized from tracking, and you can still play everything.
  • You can change this at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Purchases (Apple)

Optional in-app purchases (outfits and Platinum Picks) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best times, groove scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Sharing

If you share a daily challenge code with a friend, the code only contains the venue, star, rules and your time. It is sent through the share sheet you choose. We do not receive it.

Children

Beat Idol is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because progress is stored on your device, deleting the app removes it. To reset advertising data, use iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Apple Advertising, or reset your advertising identifier.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
