Cravelog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Cravelog stores everything you enter, including your quit date, smoking baseline, pack price, cravings, triggers and notes, only on your iPhone. The app does not create accounts, does not send data to any server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not share data with third parties. We do not collect, see or sell your information.

Because the data never leaves your device, deleting the app or using "Delete all cravings" or "Start a new plan" in Settings removes it. Backups made by iOS or iCloud follow your own device backup settings.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
