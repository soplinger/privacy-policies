Gigstub Privacy Policy

Gigstub stores everything you enter, including artists, venues, cities, dates, ticket prices, fees, ratings and setlists, only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device storage. The app does not create accounts, does not connect to any server, does not send your data anywhere, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. Deleting the app deletes its data. Because nothing leaves your device, we cannot recover data for you, so back up your iPhone if you want to keep your logs. This policy may change over time, and any change will appear in this file.
