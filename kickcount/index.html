Kickcount privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Kickcount stores everything you enter (session times, kick times, notes, your target and window settings, an optional due date, and reminder settings) only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Kickcount does not have accounts, does not send your data to any server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not share data with third parties. We cannot see your data.

Notifications are optional. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS for permission to send notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device.

To remove your data, delete individual sessions, use Delete all sessions in Settings, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. If you keep backups of your iPhone, those backups follow Apple's backup settings.

Kickcount is not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions or give medical advice. If you have concerns about your baby's movements, contact your healthcare provider or maternity unit immediately.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
