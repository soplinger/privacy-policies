Caltag Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Caltag stores everything you enter, including instrument details, reference and measured values, check dates, and job names, only on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData framework. Caltag does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests. You can delete any record inside the app at any time, and deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. If you have questions, contact the app's developer through the App Store listing.
