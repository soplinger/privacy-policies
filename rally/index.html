Rally Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Rally is designed to keep your data on your device.

- What Rally stores: the player names, match results, dates and rating values you enter. This data is stored only on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. - What Rally does not do: Rally does not create accounts, does not send your data to any server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. - Third parties: Rally shares no data with third parties. - Deleting your data: remove individual players and matches in the app, use Settings → Delete All Players and Matches, or delete the app to remove everything it stores. - Children: Rally does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. - Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the URL above. - Contact: questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the App Store listing.
