Tipjar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Tipjar stores your workplaces, shifts, tip amounts, and settings only on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not transmit your data to the developer or to anyone else, and it makes no network requests.

You can delete any shift or workplace inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal information. If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
