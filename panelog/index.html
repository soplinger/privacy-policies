Panelog Privacy Policy

Panelog stores the information you enter (array names, sizes, dates, expected yields, generation readings, cleanings and settings) only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData. Panelog does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests. You can delete all of your data at any time by deleting the app.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
